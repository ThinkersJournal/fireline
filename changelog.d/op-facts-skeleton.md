New module `fireline_contract::op_facts`: a skeleton for KISS-CONTRACT 6.3-0006 (the cell class of the `accept_predicate` is supported by the op the contract names). `check_cell_class_supported(accept_predicate, ops, &dyn OpFactSource)` decides `ctr`, `ndx`, `rdc` from facts the op declares (KISS-OPS 6.13-0009, 6.11-0012, 6.19-0036); `ew1`..`ew3` and `oth` impose nothing; a fusion passes if any op of its DAG has the fact. The crate carries NO per-op table: the facts come from an `OpFactSource`. `FactTable::parse_tsv` is a provisional loader used by the tests, to be replaced by KISS's op-facts sidecar loader when kiss ships one (asked by the PM 2026-11-15). Additive; no existing behaviour changes. New tests `tests/op_facts.rs` run over a fixture table (illustrative op names, not KISS-Ops data).
