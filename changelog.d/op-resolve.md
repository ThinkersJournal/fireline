kind: `fireline-contract::op_resolve` resolves ops to the primitive floor (6.4-0005, 6.4-0010) over KISS's op_decomposition.json
---
New module `fireline_contract::op_resolve`: `DecompTable::from_json` reads KISS's `op_decomposition.json` (schema `kiss-op-decomposition-v1`, KISS#833, kiss b84ca69, 0.18.9; no decomposition is kept in the crate). `resolve(op, unavailable)` answers `Resolved`, `Deferred(smallest unavailable op)` or `Decline` (a name outside KISS-Ops), keeping an unavailable contract distinct from a non-resolvable op (6.4-0010); a floor op needs no contract. `walk` recomputes `(level, floor)` from the `direct` edges and `check_recorded` compares it to the table's own columns; `from_json` refuses a dangling edge or one that does not strictly lower the level. `resolve_dag` applies it to every `Op` node of an `OpDag` (any decline wins, else the smallest deferred name; this aggregation is ours, not a KISS vector). Additive and not wired into `read_contract`: an unavailable contract is not a decline, so no document is newly declined. Verified: the vendored file is byte-identical to kiss b84ca69, the recorded level/floor of all 128 ops equal the walk, KISS's 11 outcome vectors (resolved, deferred, decline all present) are reproduced, a mutated level and a dangling edge are caught (anchors asserted to match once); tests, clippy `-D warnings`.
