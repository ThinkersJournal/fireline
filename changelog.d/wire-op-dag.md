kind: **BREAKING**: `read_contract` runs the Semantics `op_dag` layer and takes the KISS-Ops op facts and dtype widths
---
**Who is affected:** every caller of `fireline_contract::read::read_contract`, and every producer of KISS-Contract documents read through it. **What changed:** `read_contract(bytes, supported_versions)` becomes `read_contract(bytes, supported_versions, &dyn OpFactSource, &dyn DtypeSource)`, and after the Identity layer it runs `op_dag::check_semantics`: `semantics_kind` is one of the two values; the `op_dag` is well formed (children strictly earlier, one root, last; `Source` nodes spelled exactly, a `const`'s `dtype` a dtype token with `bits` zero-padded to its width); every `Op` name is a KISS-Ops op; the root is the op `op_identity` names (6.4-0002); and the cell class of the `accept_predicate` is met by some `Op` node (6.3-0006: `ctr` a contraction, `ndx` the index-operand role, `rdc` a declared reduce axis). A `machine-checkable-IR` document with an empty `op_dag` is declined; a `declared-op-tag` one may be empty and is then exempt. New `ReadError::Semantics(SemanticsError)`, and `Checked::op_dag`. These documents were accepted before and are declined now: an unknown or attribute-carrying op name, a second root, a `const` of the wrong width, a root that is not the identity's op, a cell class no op supports, an empty `machine-checkable-IR` DAG. **Migration:** pass the two tables, or take the one-line path `KissTables::from_json(op_facts_json, dtype_manifest_json)?.read_contract(bytes, versions)` (new module `kiss_tables`); vendor `conformance/corpus/op_facts.json` and `dtype_manifest.json` from KISS, since the crate bakes in no KISS data. A match on `ReadError` needs an arm for `Semantics` (the enum is `#[non_exhaustive]`). Verified: KISS's 39 `op_dag_vectors` through the whole reader (declines arrive as `ReadError::Semantics` with the vector's section and tag), the golden document and KISS's `section_vectors` still pass, tests, clippy `-D warnings`.
